## Classic Room

Our smallest and best-value room: a comfortable double overlooking the
courtyard, 18 m2. Everything you need for a short stay - nothing you are
paying for and not using.

## River View Room

The same comfortable double, with a small balcony facing the river and about
6 m2 more space. The one guests ask to come back to.

## Deluxe Suite

A separate sitting area alongside the bedroom, river view, and enough space
for a third guest on the sofa bed. Popular with anyone staying more than a
couple of nights.

## Aurora Suite

Two connected rooms and a private terrace - our largest room, and the one we
put families or a longer stay into when it is free. Ask early; there are only
two.
