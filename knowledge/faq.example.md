## What time can I check in?

From 15:00. If you arrive earlier we will hold your bags at reception and call
you as soon as the room is ready.

## Do you have parking?

Underground parking on site, EUR 18 a night, height limit 2.0 metres.

## Is breakfast included?

It depends on the rate you booked. If it is not included you can add it at
reception, EUR 18 per person. Children under 12 eat free. Served 07:30 to
10:30.

## Can I book a table at Aurora Kitchen?

Yes - tell us the date, time and number of guests and we will book it for
you. Aurora Kitchen is closed on Mondays; we will offer you the nearest other
evening if you ask for one.

## Can I book the spa?

Yes. Tell us the treatment (or describe what you are after) and how many
people, and we will check the time you want and book it. The Aurora Couples
Ritual is priced for two people together, not per person.

## What are the wine tasting / yoga / after-work events?

Those are bookable experiences run alongside the hotel - wine tasting on
Fridays, sunrise yoga Tuesdays and Saturdays, an after-work social on the
first Thursday of the month, and a rooftop party on Saturdays in season. We
check live spots and book you in.

## Can I cancel my booking?

Free up to 48 hours before your arrival date. Inside 48 hours we charge the
first night. If you booked through an agency, cancel with them rather than us.

## Do you allow pets?

Dogs up to 10 kg are welcome in Classic and River View rooms, EUR 15 a night.
Please tell us before you arrive. We do not have a policy for other pets -
that needs a person to check.

## How far is the airport?

About 20 minutes by taxi, usually EUR 20 to EUR 25. There is also a metro;
take it to Example station and walk five minutes.

## Can I get a late check-out?

Check-out is 11:00. We can usually offer 14:00 for EUR 30 if the room is not
needed. Ask at reception on the morning of departure.

## Is the hotel accessible?

Yes. There is a lift to every floor and two ground floor rooms have roll-in
showers. Tell us what you need when you book and we will make sure it works.

## I have a food allergy - can you tell the kitchen?

Yes, tell us the allergy and, if you have one, your booking reference, and we
will note it on your booking and pass it straight to the kitchen team.
