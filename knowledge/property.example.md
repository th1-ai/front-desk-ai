## The basics

- Name: Hotel Aurora
- Address: 1 Example Street, 1000-001 Lisbon, Portugal
- Phone: +351 200 000 000
- Email: reservations@example.com
- Website: https://example.com
- 42 rooms across 4 floors, lift to all floors
- Reception staffed 07:00 to 23:00; night porter after that

## Arrival and departure

- Check-in from 15:00. Check-out by 11:00.
- Early check-in: subject to availability, free before 13:00 is not guaranteed.
- Late check-out to 14:00: EUR 30 when the room is free, ask on the day.
- Luggage storage: free, at reception, on the day of arrival or departure.
- Getting here: 20 minutes by taxi from the airport, roughly EUR 20. Metro to
  Example station, then a 5 minute walk.
- Underground parking, height limit 2.0 metres, EUR 18 a night.

## Rooms

| Room type | Sleeps | Notes |

| Classic Room | 2 | 18 m2, courtyard view |
| River View Room | 2 | 24 m2, river-facing, small balcony |
| Deluxe Suite | 3 | 34 m2, separate sitting area, river view |
| Aurora Suite | 4 | 48 m2, two rooms, private terrace |

- All rooms: air conditioning, free wifi, safe, kettle, hairdryer.
- Cots and bottle warmers free of charge, on request. Extra bed EUR 35 a night.
- We are a non-smoking building, including balconies and terraces.
- We cannot guarantee a specific room number, only a room type.
- Dogs up to 10 kg are welcome in Classic and River View rooms, EUR 15 a
  night, please tell us in advance. There is no policy for other pets - treat
  any other pet request as something to check with a duty manager, do not
  invent an answer.

## Food and drink

- Breakfast 07:30 to 10:30, EUR 18 per person, children under 12 free.
- **Aurora Kitchen**, our restaurant, is open 07:00 to 22:30, **closed on
  Mondays**. Window tables can be pre-booked. Large parties (10 or more)
  always need a person to arrange.
- Room service: light menu, 18:00 to 22:00.

- Accessibility: two ground floor rooms with roll-in showers; the lift takes a

## Facilities

- Laundry: same-day service if handed in before 09:00.
- Bike storage in the courtyard, free.

## Things we get asked that we cannot do

- We cannot store luggage overnight for guests who are not staying that night.
- We do not run a shuttle to the airport.
- We cannot hold a table, room or spa slot without a booking reference - a
  verbal request to "keep something free" needs a real booking.
